MEET THE SELLER

Hi,
I'm Lea.

Jan Wisesa Kailea Adiningrum — although honestly, just call me Lea. I'm a freelance model, a little spoiled, and very easily convinced that I absolutely need something just because it's pretty.

I like dressing up, pretty bags, pink things, expensive little objects, and buying things for very specific occasions that somehow never happen again. Most of these pieces have been part of a shoot, an event, a trip, an endorsement, or one of those “I deserve this” moments. Some I loved, some I barely wore, and some I bought simply because I was bored.

Eventually, my closet started looking less like a closet and more like a storage unit with better lighting. So here we are.

Everything on KaileaSell has a story. I'm just ready for someone else to continue it.

Still curious about me? Catch me on X where I overshare, complain about my closet, and occasionally make another questionable purchase.

@junrnals — you know where to find me. 🎀

— Lea ♡
